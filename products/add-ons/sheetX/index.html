Google OAuth Permissions and Data Access

SheetX acts as an embedded apps script inside your current Google Sheets™ file. To provide seamless functionality specific permission to access your data.


We do not store, track, or transmit your personal information or spreadsheet contents to any external servers.


Google Sheets™
Scope: https://www.googleapis.com/auth/spreadsheets.currentonly

SheetX uses this permission to read and modify data in Google Sheet™ where the add-on is currently being used. SheetX does not request access to your other Google Sheets™ files in Google Drive™.

Google Sheets UI
Scope: https://www.googleapis.com/auth/script.container.ui

SheetX uses this permission to add menus and show dialogs for confirmation prompts, and informational pages including the About dialog, End User License Agreement (EULA), and other helpful contents.

Frequently Asked Questions

What do I need to use SheetX?

You need a Google account and access to Google Sheets™. No additional software is required.

Does SheetX with Microsoft Excel?

No, SheetX is built exclusively for Google Sheet™. It leverages specific Google ecosystem features and automation scripts that will not function in Excel.

Do I need to install anything outside Google Sheets™

No. SheetX works directly inside Google Sheets™. Install the add-on, authorize the requested permissions, and start using its functions in your spreadsheets.

Does SheetX work with my existing spreadsheets

Yes. SheetX is designed to work with the Google Sheet you are currently using. You can use its functions alongside your existing formulas and data.

Is my data shared with anyone?

No. Your data remains in your own Google Sheet and is not shared with SheetX.

Is SheetX free?

Yes. SheetX is free to use.

How do I get a feature not supported by SheetX

If you have a feature not supported by either Google Sheets or SheetX, you can contact our support team at support@eigendigital.co
