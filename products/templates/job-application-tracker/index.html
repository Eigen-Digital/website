Google OAuth Permissions and Data Access

The Job Application Tracker acts as a local productivity tool inside your Google Workspace ecosystem. To provide seamless functionality such as syncing interviews to your calendar and updating your dashboard, the application requires specific permission to access your data.


We do not store, track, or transmit your personal information or spreadsheet contents to any external servers.


Here is a transparent breakdown of exactly why each permission is requested and how it is used:

Google Sheets
Scope: https://www.googleapis.com/auth/spreadsheets

The application uses this scope manage the information you enter into your Job Application Tracker. This includes creating, reading, updating, and deleting records such as job applications, companies, contacts, interview details, and application statuses.

The application also generates supporting information, such as job IDs and timestamps, and can clear application data when you choose to reset the tracker.

Google Calendar
Scope: https://www.googleapis.com/auth/calendar

If you enable calendar synchronization, the application creates and manages interview events in a dedicated JAT Google Calendar. This helps you keep interviews, follow-ups, and other job-related events synchronized with your Google Calendar.

Google ScriptApp
Scope: https://www.googleapis.com/auth/script.scriptapp

The application uses Google Apps Script to automate routine tasks. This includes creating an onOpen trigger to initialize the spreadsheet interface whenever you open it and an hourly trigger to keep your Google Calendar synchronized automatically when calendar sync is enabled.

Google Sheets UI
Scope: https://www.googleapis.com/auth/script.container.ui

The application customizes the Google Sheets interface by adding menus and dialogs. These components provide access to application features, confirmation prompts for actions such as resetting data, and informational pages including the About dialog, End User License Agreement (EULA), and other help content.

Frequently Asked Questions

What do I need to use this template?

You need a Google account and access to Google Sheets. No additional software is required.

Does this work with Microsoft Excel?

No, this template is built exclusively for Google Sheets. It leverages specific Google ecosystem features and automation scripts that will not function in Excel.

Is this a Google Sheets template or a standalone app?

This product is a Google Sheets template enhanced with Google Apps Script. It run directly within your Google account.

Can I track multiple job applications?

Yes. The template is designed to manage multiple applications, interviews, follow-ups, and offers simultaneously.

Do I need advanced spreadsheet skills?

No. The template is designed with a little knowledge about Google Sheets and includes instructions to help you get started quickly.

Can I customize the template?

Yes, you can modify fields, statuses, categories, and other parts of the spreadsheet to fit your worflow.

Does Google Calendar integration require additional setup?

You may need to grant permission for the template to create and manage interview events in your Google Calendar.

Is my data shared with anyone?

No. Your job search information remains in your own Google account is not shared with the template creator.

Does the template work on mobile devices?

Yes. You can view and update your job applications using the Google Sheets mobile app. Some automation and setup features may work best on desktop.
