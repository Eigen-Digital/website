Privacy Policy

Effective Date: 15th June, 2026

Eigen Digital ("we," "us," or "our") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your information.

This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you purchase and use the Job Application Tracker Google Sheets template (the "Product")

1. Data Collection and Usage

Because the Product is a Google Sheets template copied directly to your personal Google account, it operates primarily within your Google Workspace environment. However, to power its advanced features, maintain code integrity and fixes any reported bugs, the template's embedded Google Apps Script communicates with the Eigen Digital Tracker (JAT), a secure editor add-on managed by Eigen Digital. All information you input—including job roles, offer tracking details, contacts, and personal notes—remains strictly within your own Google account and is subject to Google's Privacy Policy.

1.1 JAT Editor Add-on

The template's embedded Google Apps Script securely interacts with JAT, as an installed editor add-on managed by Eigen Digital.

  • Stateless Processing (No Storage): When you execute a custom function or menu item, the JAT add-on uses the necessary Google services to run the requested logic. It does not collect, store, log, or retain any of your personal information, email addresses, or spreadsheet data. Once the function is executed and the result is sent back to your Google Sheet, the data is instantly dropped from memory.
  • Google Permissions: The permissions requested during initialization (Google Sheets, Google Calendar, ScriptApp, etc.) are used solely to allow the local template to read your inputs, pass them to the JAT add-on for processing, and apply the resulting automation back to your personal Google account.
  • Zero Third-Party Sharing: Because we do not store any data processed by JAT, your information can never be shared, sold, or accessed by third parties.

2. Google Apps Script and Permissions

The Job Application Management System utilizes embedded Google Apps Script to automate workflows (such as formatting, generating calendar events for interviews, and custom menu interfaces). To function, the script requires you to grant specific Google account permissions (OAuth scopes).


When you authorize the script, you are granting the script access to perform actions strictly within your own Google Account. The specific permissions required are:

Google Sheets (currentonly) View and manage spreadsheets that this application has been installed in.

This permisson allows the product to:

  • Create, read, update, and delete job application records.
  • Insert generated values such as Job ID and job creation timestamp into designated cells within the spreadsheet.
  • Clear content such as applications, companies, and contacts when you explicitly choose to reset the product.
Google Calendar See, edit, share, and permanently delete all the calendars you can access using Google Calendar.

This permisson is required only if you enable calendar synchronization. It allows the product to:

  • Create interview events in a dedicated JAT calendar.
  • Update interview titles, dates, and times when changes are made within the spreadsheet.
  • Delete interview events when they are deleted from the application to synchronization is disabled.
  • Keep your interview schedule synchronized between the spreadsheet and Google Calendar.

The application does not modify unrelated calendar events or calendars that are not associated with Job Application Tracker.

Google ScriptApp Allow this application to run when you are not present.

This permission allows the application to install and manage Google Apps Script triggers required for automation.
These triggers are used to:

  • Initialize the product interface whenever you open the spreadsheet.
  • Automatically synchronize interview event with Google Calendar on a scheduled bases.
Google Sheets UI Display and run third-party web content in prompts and sidebars inside Google applications.

This permission allows the application to display custom user interface components within Google Sheets, including:

  • Custom menus that extends the product funtionality.
  • Dialog windows to prompt users for confirmation when resetting the product.
  • Informational dialog that shows more about the producy and help documentation.
External Service Request Connect to an external service.

This permission allows the application to send external request to the Eigen Digital's License Verification Service with the following data:

  • User's email address to verify license.
  • The application receives only the license verification result to enable features; no Google Sheets or Google Calendar data is transmitted through this request.

We do not sell, rent, or share user information with third parties for advertising purposes. Any data accessed through Google services is used solely to provide the functionality of the Job Application Tracker.
