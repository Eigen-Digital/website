Frequently Asked Questions

What do I need to use this template?

You need a Google account and access to Google Sheets. No additional software is required.

Does this work with Microsoft Excel?

No, this template is built exclusively for Google Sheets. It leverages specific Google ecosystem features and automation scripts that will not function in Excel.

Is this a Google Sheets template or a standalone app?

This product is a Google Sheets template enhanced with Google Apps Script. It run directly within your Google account.

Can I track multiple job applications?

Yes. The template is designed to manage multiple applications, interviews, follow-ups, and offers simultaneously.

Do I need advanced spreadsheet skills?

No. The template is designed with a little knowledge about Google Sheets and includes instructions to help you get started quickly.

Can I customize the template>

Yes, you can modify fields, statuses, categories, and other parts of the spreadsheet to fit your worflow.

Does Google Calendar integration require additional setup?

You may need to grant permission for the template to create and manage interview events in your Google Calendar.

Is my data shared with anyone?

No. Your job search information remains in your own Google account is not shared with the template creator.

Does the template work on mobile devices?

Yes. You can view and update your job applications using the Google Sheets mobile app. Some automation and setup features may work best on desktop.
